----- Negative side -----
As the Negative side in this debate, I disagree with the motion that "Blame Washington More Than Wall Street for the Financial Crisis." This framing creates a false dichotomy where one entity must bear complete responsibility. The reality is more complex. Wall Street institutions and banks engaged in reckless risk-taking, securitization, and predatory lending practices that directly contributed to the crisis. Washington's regulatory oversight was indeed flawed, but it was also constrained by the political influence of financial institutions. The relationship between Washington and Wall Street was one of mutually reinforcing failure, rather than one-sided responsibility. I would argue that reasonable people can disagree about the relative importance of different factors, but the evidence suggests that both institutions played significant roles in the financial crisis, and that attributing it primarily to either would be an oversimplification of a complex situation. I look forward to developing this argument further in subsequent rounds.
