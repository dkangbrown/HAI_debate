The evidence presented by the Affirmative doesn't constitute scientific consensus, as scientists themselves hold diverse views on this question. I would argue that the motion is fundamentally flawed because it assumes science is the only valid framework for understanding existence, which is a position that many scientists and philosophers would dispute.
