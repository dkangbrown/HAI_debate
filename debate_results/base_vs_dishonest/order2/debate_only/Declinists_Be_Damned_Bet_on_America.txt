===== Debate Round-2 =====

----- Affirmative side -----
To respond to the Negative side's claims, let's analyze the most critical points they raised and address them with new evidence and perspectives.

Firstly, the Negative side emphasizes that America is experiencing the worst period of economic growth in modern history. However, this argument relies on a narrow definition of economic growth. They fail to account for the significant gains in productivity, technological advancements, and innovative sectors like renewable energy, biotechnology, and cybersecurity. These areas are not only driving economic growth but also positioning the United States as a leader in emerging industries.

Moreover, the Negative side's emphasis on infrastructure collapse is misleading. While it is true that some bridges are in disrepair, the vast majority of our nation's infrastructure is in good condition. According to the American Society of Civil Engineers, the country's overall infrastructure grade is a C+, with 75% of bridges rated as safe or satisfactory. This is not a crisis, but rather an opportunity for targeted investments to upgrade and modernize our infrastructure.

The Negative side also claims that other developed nations have surpassed America in all meaningful metrics. However, this assertion is based on a flawed comparison. They fail to account for the unique strengths and challenges of each nation. For instance, countries like Japan and Germany have aging populations and shrinking workforces
